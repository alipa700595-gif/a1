Craftsmanship & Fiber Disclaimer

Essential information regarding natural wool characteristics, screen color reproduction, and laundering guidelines.

1. Natural Wool Characteristics & Lanolin

100% natural Merino wool contains microscopic lanolin oils that provide natural water repellency, dirt resistance, and antibacterial properties. When freshly unboxed, our raw worsted wool socks may emit a faint, wholesome natural sheep's wool aroma. This scent is a natural hallmark of un-bleached, minimally processed fibers and dissipates naturally after initial gentle laundering.

2. Botanical Dye Variations & Lightfastness

Our botanical dye baths utilize wild-harvested roots, barks, and leaves. Unlike synthetic petrochemical dyes that provide flat, artificial uniformity, natural botanical dyes produce living, multi-tonal heathering. Subtle color depth differences between dye kettle batches are expected and celebrated as marks of genuine artisan handcraft.

3. Digital Display & Color Accuracy

While we photograph all knitwear under calibrated 5500K daylight lighting conditions, the exact visual warmth and hue of natural yarn dyes may vary depending on individual monitor calibrations, smartphone screen color temperatures, and ambient viewing lighting.

4. Laundering & Thermal Shrinkage

Wool is a protein fiber with microscopic overlapping cuticle scales. Exposing wool socks to excessive hot water washing (>40°C) or high-heat tumble dryers will cause mechanical felting and permanent thermal shrinkage. Always follow our cold wool wash and flat air-drying protocols to maintain original elasticity and sizing.

5. Editorial & Podiatric Information

All articles, foot health recommendations, and thermal guidelines published within The Alpine Knit Journal (warmweavehill.com/blog) are provided for general educational and lifestyle purposes only. They do not constitute formal medical or podiatric advice.
