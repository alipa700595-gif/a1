Data Governance & Ethics

Privacy & Data Protection Policy

Last updated: January 2026 • Strict adherence to the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Commitment to User Privacy

At Warm Weave Hill ("the Mill", "we", "our", or "us"), we value your privacy with the same uncompromising integrity we apply to our artisanal woolcraft. This Privacy Policy sets forth how we collect, handle, process, store, and safeguard your personal information when you visit our website (warmweavehill.com), purchase our knitwear, subscribe to our newsletter, or communicate with our trail concierge.

2. Types of Information Collected

We collect only the essential personal details required to deliver handcrafted knitwear and exceptional client service:

  • Contact & Identification Data: Full name, shipping address, billing address, telephone number, and email address supplied during checkout or inquiry submission.
  • Knitwear & Order Preferences: Sock size selections, cushioning preferences, fiber blend favorites, and past purchasing history.
  • Payment Information: Encrypted payment tokens, billing postal codes, and transaction confirmation IDs processed through PCI-DSS certified payment gateways. We do not store raw credit card numbers on our servers.
  • Telemetry & Analytics Data: IP address, device type, browser platform, session duration, and referral paths collected anonymously via Google Analytics (G-0LY0HY7L01).

3. Legal Bases for Data Processing

Under Article 6 of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), we process your data under the following lawful grounds:

  1. Contractual Execution: To fulfill knitwear orders, process payments, manufacture custom batches, and ship products to your delivery address.
  2. Legitimate Interests: To maintain our lifetime warranty records, combat fraud, and optimize website stability.
  3. Legal Compliance: To satisfy mandatory statutory accounting, tax, customs, and export compliance obligations under Austrian and EU law.
  4. Consent: To deliver seasonal hearth newsletter dispatches when you explicitly opt in to our mailing club.

4. Data Retention & Security Standards

We maintain customer order records in an encrypted environment for the duration necessary to honor our Lifetime Trail & Hearth Warranty. General analytics and promotional marketing data is retained strictly in accordance with European statutory retention schedules and purged regularly.

5. Third-Party Sharing Restrictions

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal data with commercial advertisers or third-party brokers. Personal data is shared solely with vetted sub-processors essential to fulfilling your orders:

  • Carbon-neutral international courier partners (e.g. Austrian Post, DHL Express).
  • PCI-DSS Level 1 certified payment processing networks.
  • Secure cloud hosting providers located entirely within the European Economic Area (EEA).

6. Your Statutory Rights as a Data Subject

Under international privacy laws (GDPR, CCPA), you possess the following actionable rights:

  • The right to request a complete copy of all personal data held about you.
  • The right to rectify inaccurate, outdated, or incomplete records.
  • The right to request data erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"), subject to statutory tax record retention requirements.
  • The right to withdraw marketing consent at any time with immediate effect.
  • The right to lodge a formal complaint with the Austrian Data Protection Authority (Datenschutzbehörde).

7. Contact Our Data Protection Officer

To exercise your privacy rights or submit questions regarding data handling, please contact our Data Protection Officer at:
