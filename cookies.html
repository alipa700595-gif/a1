Digital Transparency

Cookie & Tracking Technology Policy

Transparent information regarding cookies, local storage tokens, and telemetry controls.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small data files placed on your browser or device when you interact with digital websites. At Warm Weave Hill, we respect your digital privacy. We utilize cookies strictly to ensure seamless website functionality, remember your interactive Warmth Studio selections, and review anonymized aggregate visitor traffic.

2. Categories of Cookies Utilized

Our website utilizes three discrete categories of digital tokens:

  • Strictly Necessary Cookies: Essential for fundamental platform operations, shopping cart persistence, load balancing, and security. These cannot be disabled.
  • Functional Cookies: Store your interactive Warmth & Cushioning Studio configurations while you navigate between pages.
  • Analytical Cookies: We utilize Google Analytics (tag: G-0LY0HY7L01) to understand aggregate page visits, geographic trends, and device resolutions. All visitor IP addresses are automatically anonymized before processing.

4. Managing and Disabling Cookies

You have complete control over cookies on your computer or mobile device. You can configure your browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge) to block third-party cookies or delete stored browsing cache at any time.

5. Policy Updates & Contact

This policy may be revised periodically to adhere to evolving data regulations. For questions regarding our cookie practices, please contact: privacy@warmweavehill.com.
